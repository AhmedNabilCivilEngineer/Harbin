Company Overview

Harbin Modern Construction is a general contracting and civil engineering firm based in Alexandria, Egypt. The company delivers integrated construction services covering full-scope contracting, structural design, BIM modeling, and project controls with Primavera P6.

Led by Eng. Ahmed Nabil (GMICE, UK · Egyptian Syndicate of Engineers), the firm focuses on precise structural engineering combined with disciplined cost and schedule control from design through handover.

Core Services

General Contracting

Full-scope delivery for residential, commercial, and infrastructure projects — mobilization to handover.

Project Controls

Primavera P6 CPM scheduling, WBS, Earned Value Management (EVA), and cash-flow forecasting.

BIM & Structural Modeling

3D Revit modeling, FEM analysis, foundation framing, and rebar detailing.

Structural Analysis

Robot Structural Analysis, flat-slab punching shear checks, and PLAXIS 3D soil-foundation modeling.
